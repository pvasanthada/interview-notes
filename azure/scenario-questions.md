# Azure — Scenario Questions

**Label:** `Interview Preparation`

Use the [Scenario Framework](../docs/answering-frameworks.md#2-scenario-framework) for all of these.

## Foundation
1. Explain how you'd set up a new Azure subscription for a small team, covering identity, budget, and basic guardrails.
2. A resource group has resources nobody can identify the owner of. How do you investigate and resolve?

## Intermediate
3. A team's App Service deployment intermittently fails with 502s during traffic spikes. Walk through your diagnostic approach.
4. Design connectivity between two Azure VNets in different subscriptions that also need on-prem access.

## Senior
5. Your Azure spend has grown 40% quarter over quarter with no corresponding usage growth. Diagnose and remediate.
6. Design a landing zone strategy for a company migrating from a single flat subscription to a multi-subscription enterprise model.
7. A production Key Vault outage in one region took down authentication for multiple dependent apps. Redesign for resilience.

## Principal
8. Design an Azure platform for a healthcare company requiring HIPAA compliance, multi-region DR, and strict data residency.
9. Your organization is being acquired and must integrate its Azure tenant with the acquiring company's tenant within 6 months. Plan the approach.
10. Leadership wants to reduce Azure spend by 25% without degrading reliability. Build the plan.

## Architecture Scenario
11. Design a secure, governed self-service platform allowing 50 application teams to provision their own Azure resources within guardrails, without granting them subscription Owner access.
12. Design a multi-region active-passive architecture for a financial transaction system with a 15-minute RTO and near-zero RPO requirement.
