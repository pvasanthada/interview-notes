# Interview Preparation Roadmap

**Label:** `Interview Preparation`

A phased, multi-week study plan through this repository. Adjust the pacing to your available time — this assumes roughly 4–6 weeks of part-time study.

## Phase 1 — Foundation (Week 1)
Cloud fundamentals, networking, IAM basics.

- [ ] [`networking/tcp-ip.md`](networking/tcp-ip.md), [`networking/dns.md`](networking/dns.md), [`networking/routing.md`](networking/routing.md)
- [ ] [`azure/identity-rbac-pim.md`](azure/identity-rbac-pim.md), [`gcp/iam.md`](gcp/iam.md)
- [ ] [`docs/answering-frameworks.md`](docs/answering-frameworks.md), [`docs/principal-architect-mindset.md`](docs/principal-architect-mindset.md)

## Phase 2 — Senior (Weeks 2–3)
Landing zones, Kubernetes, Terraform, security.

- [ ] [`azure/landing-zones.md`](azure/landing-zones.md), [`azure/networking.md`](azure/networking.md), [`gcp/landing-zones.md`](gcp/landing-zones.md), [`gcp/networking.md`](gcp/networking.md)
- [ ] Full [`kubernetes/`](kubernetes/) folder
- [ ] Full [`terraform/`](terraform/) folder
- [ ] Full [`security/`](security/) folder

## Phase 3 — Principal (Week 4)
Architecture, governance, platform engineering, FinOps.

- [ ] Full [`architecture/`](architecture/) folder
- [ ] Full [`platform-engineering/`](platform-engineering/) folder
- [ ] [`adr/`](adr/) — review sample ADRs and write one of your own for a real past decision

## Phase 4 — AI Architect (Week 5)
RAG, agents, MCP, LLMOps, AI security — with honest maturity framing.

- [ ] Full [`ai-genai/`](ai-genai/) folder — re-read the [maturity disclaimer](ai-genai/README.md) and decide how you'll frame your own actual depth
- [ ] Practice explaining RAG and agent architecture out loud in under 3 minutes each

## Phase 5 — Interview Simulation (Week 6)
Scenario questions, whiteboarding, leadership and behavioral.

- [ ] Work through [`scenarios/`](scenarios/) — practice each out loud using the Scenario Framework, not just reading the worked answer
- [ ] Full [`leadership/`](leadership/) folder — prepare your own STAR stories, not just the worked examples
- [ ] Run at least 2 of the [`mock-interviews.md`](mock-interviews.md) rounds with a peer or by recording yourself
- [ ] Final pass through [`quick-revision.md`](quick-revision.md)
