# Platform Engineering — Scenario Questions

**Label:** `Interview Preparation`

## Foundation
1. What's the difference between DevOps and Platform Engineering, and why has the latter emerged as a distinct discipline?
2. Explain what "platform as a product" means in practice.

## Intermediate
3. A new microservice currently takes 6 weeks of manual back-and-forth to provision. Redesign the process.
4. How would you decide what belongs in a golden path versus what teams should decide for themselves?

## Senior
5. Adoption of your platform's golden path is low despite it being "the recommended approach." Diagnose and fix.
6. Design a self-service infrastructure platform that avoids granting application teams direct cloud subscription/project access.
7. How would you measure whether your platform investment is actually improving developer velocity?

## Principal
8. Design a self-service cloud platform that allows developers to provision compliant infrastructure without requiring direct access to cloud subscriptions/projects. (See [`self-service.md`](self-service.md) for the full worked answer.)
9. Leadership questions platform team headcount, viewing it as overhead rather than an accelerant. Build the business case.
10. Design an internal developer platform strategy for an organization with 200 engineers and no existing golden paths.

## Architecture Scenario
11. Design the end-to-end architecture, workflow, governance, and trade-offs of a platform allowing 50 application teams to self-service provision Kubernetes workloads with security guardrails enforced automatically.
12. Propose a phased, multi-quarter roadmap for building a platform engineering capability from scratch in an organization currently relying on a central, ticket-based infrastructure team.
