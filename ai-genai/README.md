## Important Labeling Disclaimer

This is the fastest-moving and most hype-prone domain in this repository. Every file below is explicitly labeled, and the labels are load-bearing — **do not represent this material as production operational experience in an interview unless it genuinely is for you.** Most content here is `Reference Architecture` (a documented design pattern for reasoning purposes) or `Learning / Overview` (conceptual understanding), not `Production Experience`.

## How to Talk About This Honestly in an Interview

> "I've built and tested [specific thing] in a proof-of-concept setting, and I've studied the production operational patterns — throughput/cost control, evaluation, guardrails — deeply enough to design for them, but I want to be upfront that my hands-on production ownership of GenAI infrastructure specifically is [state your actual truth]. Here's how I'd approach it architecturally..."

This kind of honesty, paired with genuine architectural depth, reads as *more* senior than overclaiming — principal-level interviewers can usually tell the difference anyway, and the honesty itself signals judgment.
